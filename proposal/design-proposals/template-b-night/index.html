The problem

Convenience
has a cost.

Keep scrolling →

01

Single-use, every meal.

Takeaway meals generate large amounts of single-use food packaging waste.

02

BYOC runs on memory.

Existing Bring-Your-Own-Container initiatives depend on students remembering to bring their own.

03

Forget it, no plan B.

Students who forget their containers have no alternative for reusable packaging. That's the gap ReBox fills.

How it works

01

Borrow

Pay a refundable $5 deposit and take a container and stainless-steel utensils from the machine.

02

Order

Pass the container to the stall owner, who packs your takeaway in it.

03

Return

Drop it at any participating canteen whose machine isn't full. OmniBus shows you where.

04

Refund

Get your $5 back anytime, as long as the container is returned.

05

Wash & move

Canteens clean containers in commercial dishwashers and redistribute them to other canteens.

Reality check

Risks, and how we'll test.

Limitations

  • 01High initial cost

    Upfront investment in reusable containers and utensils.

  • 02Low return rates

    Higher replacement costs that eat into the environmental benefits.

  • 03More washing

    More water and electricity, and possibly higher manpower costs.

  • 04Redistribution

    Containers need to be redistributed between canteens frequently.

  • 05Hygiene perception

    Users may feel containers are dirty or not cleaned properly.
