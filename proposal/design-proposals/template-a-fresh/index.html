Forgot your container? Borrow one.

A deposit-based reusable container system across NTU canteens. Borrow a container from the machine, return it at any participating canteen, and get your $5 deposit back.

The problem

Every takeaway ends in the bin.

  1. Single-use packaging piles up.Takeaway meals generate large amounts of single-use food packaging waste.
  2. BYOC relies on memory.Bring-Your-Own-Container initiatives only work if students remember to bring one.
  3. Forget it, and there's no option.Students who forget their containers have no reusable alternative.

What if forgetting didn't mean going disposable?

How it works

Borrow. Eat. Return.

  1. 01Borrow

    Pay a refundable $5 deposit and take a container and stainless-steel utensils from the machine.

  2. 02Order

    Pass the container to the stall owner, who packs your takeaway in it.

  3. 03Return

    Drop it at any participating canteen's return spot. The map shows which machines are nearby and not full.

  4. 04Refund

    Get your $5 back anytime, as long as the container has been returned.

  5. 05Wash & redistribute

    Canteens clean containers in commercial dishwashers and redistribute them to other canteens.

Limitations

What could go wrong.

01

High initial cost

Upfront investment in reusable containers and utensils.

02

Low return rates

Unreturned containers raise replacement costs and eat into the environmental benefits.

03

More washing

Extra water and electricity, and possibly higher manpower costs.

04

Redistribution

Containers need to be moved between canteens frequently.

05

Hygiene concerns

Users may feel containers are dirty or not cleaned properly.

Expected outcomes

  • Less single-use takeaway packaging.
  • Reusable packaging within reach, even for those who forget.
  • Sustainable habits through regular borrowing and returning.
  • A lower environmental impact and carbon footprint from canteens.
